# STE lite (opt-in)

Optional tip for clearer explanations. **Not** standing global policy — paste into a session or project only when you want ~80% ASD-STE100 clarity without the full standard.

## When explaining
- One meaning per word; reuse the same term for the same idea.
- Prefer short sentences. Aim ≤20 words for instructions, ≤25 for descriptions.
- One instruction or one topic per sentence.
- Active voice. Simple present, past, or imperative — avoid perfect/progressive when clarity suffers.
- Prefer concrete verbs (`start`, `check`, `remove`) over vague ones (`initiate`, `utilize`, `perform`).
- Limit noun stacks to about three words; break longer chains.
- Keep required facts, numbers, and conditions; do not drop qualifiers to sound shorter.
- Put warning or prerequisite conditions before the action they affect.

Do **not** add this snippet to standing global `AGENTS.md` / `CLAUDE.md` files.
